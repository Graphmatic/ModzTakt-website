ModzTakt

ModzTakt application and VST3 plugin let you add MIDI modulation sources to Elektron Syntakt

It proposes a syncable LFO and an Envelope Generator that can be routed to up to 3 tracks/MIDI channels, a MIDI notes Delay/Echo with step sequencers for echoed notes, also routable to up to 3 tracks/MIDI channels.

Standalone version for Linux allows to records Syntakt audio channels exposed by the Overbridge driver.

ModzTakt is available for Windows and Linux, as standalone application or VST pluggin.
